## Performance

> Manual old-vs-new benchmark of the `--columnar` engine over the set in [`columnar_wip/benchmark-networks.md`](columnar_wip/benchmark-networks.md). This is **not** the CI `perf-pr.yml` check, which only sees the default OO path since the new core is flag-gated.

Single-threaded (`MODE=release OPENMP=0`), `--seed 123`. Codelength in bits. **`instr` is instructions retired** (`/usr/bin/time -l`). `time` is `--timing-json`'s `timing.total_s`.
- `-N10` rows run once (deterministic; `instr` carries the comparison).
- `-N1` rows are the interleaved minimum of 3.
- Driver: [`columnar_wip/bench-sync.py`](columnar_wip/bench-sync.py), both engines measured (no `--carry-oo`), writing [`columnar_wip/sync-2160-ab-results.tsv`](columnar_wip/sync-2160-ab-results.tsv), old and new on every row of the #1389 snapshot.
- Re-timed: every configuration whose codelength changed, with [`columnar_wip/retime-changed.py`](columnar_wip/retime-changed.py), writing [`columnar_wip/sync-2160-retime-changed.tsv`](columnar_wip/sync-2160-retime-changed.tsv). The outliers in seconds, with [`columnar_wip/retime-outliers.py`](columnar_wip/retime-outliers.py), writing [`columnar_wip/sync-2160-retime.tsv`](columnar_wip/sync-2160-retime.tsv).
- Feature build: [`columnar_wip/bench-feature-sync.py`](columnar_wip/bench-feature-sync.py), both engines, writing [`columnar_wip/sync-2160-feature.tsv`](columnar_wip/sync-2160-feature.tsv).
- Tables: [`columnar_wip/assemble-hier-gaps.py`](columnar_wip/assemble-hier-gaps.py) builds every table from the TSV that [`columnar_wip/tables-1042.py`](columnar_wip/tables-1042.py) `assembly` writes, including the OO cells. The REG table comes from `tables-1042.py reg`.

> **This PR syncs master into `columnar-hierarchical-core`: 9 commits, through the 2.16.0 release, as merge `15b573ff`.** Three of them move results:
> - **#1395 (#1353): the flow is iterated to convergence, with no default iteration cap.** Before, the power iteration stopped at 400 steps. Under `--regularized` on a state network that left the flow unconverged: wikispeedia at an L1 change of 1e-3, air30k `-d` at 4e-6, om6/om7 at 1e-7. Master now stops at the tolerance or at the rounding floor, and accelerates slow iterations (Anderson acceleration).
> - **#1367 (#1366): an undirected run sums a link `a b` and its opposite link `b a` into one link.** This replaces the branch's count-once copies of #1203 and #1264 (F80).
> - **#1339: relaxation reaches layers whose ids are further apart than there are layers.**
>
> The merge has no conflict in `FlowCalculator.cpp`: master's convergence driver runs the branch's #828 per-layer-prior step as it is (F80). It also closes **#1374**: with the flow converged, both engines score malaria's by-layer partition at 8.264076186 bits, 7e-15 apart (it was 1.1e-7 apart at the 400-step cap).
>
> What the snapshot shows:
> - **Columnar: 309 of 332 configurations are bit-identical** in codelength, top modules and levels. Over the 272 of them above 0.3G instructions, the median instruction change is −0.02% (5th–95th percentile −2.60% to +0.07%). The faster tail is #1395's rounding-floor stop and #1367's halved lazega.
> - **All 23 columnar configurations that change are directed `--regularized` runs, all from #1395,** and all are shorter in bits:
>   - wikispeedia: **−26.9% to −27.3% in bits.** The old numbers were scored on a flow 1e-3 from stationary.
>   - air30k (reg.): −0.022% to −0.033% in bits.
>   - om6_N256_E50k and om7_N256_E100k: −0.0003% and −0.00002% in bits; the planted controls move by the same order.
>   - The old binary run with `--max-flow-iterations 200000` reproduces the new codelength exactly on wikispeedia and air30k (reg.), and to 1e-9 bits on om6.
>   - Undirected `--regularized` rows (air30k, jazz, lazega, …) are bit-identical: their flow has no power iteration.
> - **OO: 4 of 28 configurations change.**
>   - air30k `-N10`: −0.066% in bits at −10.9% in instructions, from #1367 (2,833 opposite links summed). The old binary on input with those links pre-summed reproduces it exactly. `-2 -N10`: +0.0001% in bits, from #1367 too (undirected, so no other master change reaches it).
>   - air30k (reg.) `-N10` and `-2 -N10`: +0.0011% in bits each, from #1395. The converged flow alone takes the old partition 0.033% lower, as on columnar; the OO search then lands 0.034% above that.
>   - With OO's air30k lower, columnar's `-C -N10` air30k (unchanged) now reads +0.018% in bits against OO, where it read −0.047%.
> - **Feature build:** malaria +0.0002% in bits in both engines, from the converged flow, at −5% to −24% in instructions. EU airlines −0.246% in bits in both engines, from #1339. StarWars is bit-identical at +2.2% to +9.5% in instructions, for 123 more flow steps.
> - **Seconds:** summed over the 332 columnar configurations, −0.04% in the sweep. The 23 changed configurations re-timed as the minimum of 5: +3.6% summed. The unchanged outliers re-timed land between −1.2% and +1.5% on every row above 0.5 s, so their sweep spikes (up to +36%) were load.
> - **The cost of the converged flow is concentrated in single-trial `--regularized` runs:**
>   - wikispeedia `-N1` +65% and +79% in seconds (0.09 → 0.14 s and 0.07 → 0.13 s);
>   - air30k (reg.) `-N1` +29% (0.46 → 0.59 s);
>   - om6/om7 `-N1` +2% to +9%;
>   - flow-only planted controls +43% or −23% to −26%.
>   - This is master's #1395 cost, accepted there (OO wikispeedia `-N1` +20% in instructions on master). At `-N10` it is +0.2% to +7.7%.
>
> Both engines measured in one session, 2026-10-27, interleaved per row, with no other benchmark or test suite running. The machine carried other load (load average 15–45), so instructions carry the comparison where seconds move alone.
> - **Old:** a fresh build of the branch tip `3f6328c6` (#1389), md5 `b675cde2772815d5e374e2f49fcfe358`, byte-identical to #1389's new arm. Feature build: md5 `b8ade12537af61d0a6655292b6f7e4a8`, also identical.

#### Every cell that is worse, explained

- **In bits (all under 0.1%):**
  - Feature-build malaria, +0.0002% in both engines at `-N10`. This is the objective moving, not the search. The old partition scores exactly the new number under the converged flow.
  - OO malaria `-N1` is +0.0055%: the OO search lands 0.0053% above the old partition re-scored.
  - OO air30k (reg.) +0.0011% (`-N10`, `-2 -N10`): see the opening note.
  - OO air30k `-2 -N10` +0.0001%, #1367's summed links moving OO's search.
- **In seconds, on changed rows:** every cost above is #1395's iteration to a stationary flow.
  - Each case pairs with a gain in bits, or sits on a flow-only planted control.
  - air30k (reg.) `-N1` reads +29% in seconds for +7.3% in instructions: the extra flow steps run at much lower instructions per cycle than the search. They pass over air30k's 265k links per step.
  - Several om6/om7 rows pay +0.4% to +4% in instructions for ≤0.0003% in bits. The flow they were scored on was 1e-7 from stationary; that is the trade master accepted in #1395.
- **In seconds, on bit-identical rows:** none above +1.5% once re-timed (the largest sweep spikes: om6_N256_E100k `-C -2d -N10` +35.8% → +0.9%, word_assoc (pref.) `-C -2 -N10` +21.4% → +1.5%, air30k (meta) `--regularized -N10` +20.4% → −0.5%).
  - StarWars in the feature build is the one bit-identical network that costs more: +2.2% to +9.5% in instructions on 0.14–0.8G runs.
  - Its flow stopped at the 400-step cap at 9.5e-13 and now converges in 523 plain steps. It decays at 0.946 per step, just below the 0.95 that starts acceleration.

### The overlapping family in full

Every configuration of the planted overlapping state networks: om2–om8 on N = 256 physical nodes at
E = 50k and 100k trigrams (F55), under both objectives (F66). Each block of five starts with its planted
partition, scored with `-2d --no-infomap -c` under the block's objective. Then come the four searches.
- **Δ vs planted** is the new search's codelength against that planted partition, **bold** where the
  search is worse.
- On a planted row it is the planted partition against one-level instead. Where that is **bold** the
  planted partition is worse than one-level: it is no reference, and the block's deltas are ~~struck~~.
- The plain search rows are the same runs as the `-C`, `-C -2` and `-C -N1` rows of that network.
- The `--regularized` blocks of om6_N256_E50k and om7_N256_E100k change on the converged flow (#1395): the searches by
  −0.0003% and −0.00002% in bits, their planted controls by −0.0002% and −0.0001%. The planted `--regularized`
  controls of om5_N256_E50k and om8_N256_E100k move by 1e-9 bits. Every other row is bit-identical.
