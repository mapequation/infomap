## Performance

> Manual old-vs-new benchmark of the `--columnar` engine over the set in [`columnar_wip/benchmark-networks.md`](columnar_wip/benchmark-networks.md). This is **not** the CI `perf-pr.yml` check, which only sees the default OO path since the new core is flag-gated.

Single-threaded (`MODE=release OPENMP=0`), `--seed 123`. Codelength in bits. **`instr` is instructions retired** (`/usr/bin/time -l`). `time` is `--timing-json`'s `timing.total_s`.
- `-N10` rows run once (deterministic; `instr` carries the comparison).
- `-N1` rows are the interleaved minimum of 3.
- Driver: [`columnar_wip/bench-sync.py`](columnar_wip/bench-sync.py) `--carry-oo`, writing [`columnar_wip/multilayer-prior-ab-results.tsv`](columnar_wip/multilayer-prior-ab-results.tsv), old and new on every row of the previous snapshot (#1373).
- Outliers in seconds: [`columnar_wip/retime-outliers.py`](columnar_wip/retime-outliers.py), writing [`columnar_wip/multilayer-prior-retime.tsv`](columnar_wip/multilayer-prior-retime.tsv).
- Regularized multilayer (feature build): [`columnar_wip/bench-multilayer.py`](columnar_wip/bench-multilayer.py), writing [`columnar_wip/multilayer-prior-ml-results.tsv`](columnar_wip/multilayer-prior-ml-results.tsv).
- Tables: [`columnar_wip/assemble-hier-gaps.py`](columnar_wip/assemble-hier-gaps.py) builds every table from the TSV that [`columnar_wip/tables-1042.py`](columnar_wip/tables-1042.py) `assembly` writes, including the OO cells. The REG table comes from `tables-1042.py reg`.

> **This PR adds the regularized-multilayer teleport prior to the columnar engine (#828).** Under `--regularized`, multilayer flow teleports within each layer. A module M therefore exits by teleportation at Σ_l T_{M,l}(W_l − W_{M,l}) and enters at Σ_l (T_l − T_{M,l})W_{M,l}, where T and W are teleport flow and weight. Until now `-C` optimized and reported its codelength without these terms. `ColumnarBoundaryFlow` (#1373) gains a third teleport kind, per layer, next to none and global:
> - levels carry per-unit, per-layer teleport sums;
> - `aggregate` evaluates the per-layer closed form;
> - the move and merge loops get the teleport flow between a unit and a module from a module × layer table: dense up to 2^20 cells, hashed beyond;
> - the move loop is compiled once per kind.
>
> No search code knows about layers. The finding is F74.
>
> What the snapshot shows:
> - **On multilayer input the columnar engine now codes OO's objective.** On 8 of 9 multilayer networks, each engine scores the other's partition at 0 relative error. Malaria is 8e-9 apart in both directions; the same gap is there without the prior and on the tip (#1374). The old engine's reported codelengths were not the objective: DanioRerio `-N10` reported 1.77 bits for a partition that scores 10.17 under the prior. See [the regularized multilayer section](#regularized-multilayer-828).
> - **The 332 configurations of the set are all bit-identical**, because none of them is multilayer under `--regularized`. Instructions: median +0.06%, 5th–95th percentile −0.23% to +0.32%.
> - **Seconds in this session are load, not change.** Another job held the machine at load averages of 57 to 388 (1 to 15 minutes) during the re-timing and the multilayer table, and possibly during the sweep. The 36 re-timed rows read −22% to +61% in seconds at −0.39% to +0.40% in instructions. Instructions carry the comparison here. Seconds summed: +2.55% in the sweep, −1.48% with the re-timed rows.
>
> Columnar arms measured in one session, 2026-10-22/23. OO arms carried from #1373, which carried them from #1369's session.
> - **Old:** a fresh build of the branch tip `1f8312a9` (#1373), md5 `777c20dac8bb63080f7946abf64a8741`. It is byte-identical to #1373's new arm. Feature build (for the multilayer table): md5 `360478379b73b4777592c266a59a924e`.
> - **New:** `2740ec9c`, md5 `d1da5248ea7b61b049fa3b83d834f7cf`. Feature build: md5 `ab45d66545ef7b3ce597d3395771499a`.
> - **Family rows** that repeat a `-C`, `-C -2` or `-C -N1` row take that row's run, so the two tables agree.

### Regularized multilayer (#828)

Multilayer input under `--regularized`, which needs the feature build (`FEATURES="lossy-map-equation regularized-multilayer"`), on both arms and for OO. OO runs on the new binary, since the change does not touch it.
- **Old columnar** gets two columns: the codelength it reported, which left out the prior, and its partition scored by OO under the prior (`--no-infomap -c` on the states tree). The second is the old arm's real codelength, and the new arm is compared against it.
- **Inputs:** malaria is the benchmark set's. The two toys are `examples/networks/`. The real-world networks (`networks/multilayer/real-world/`) and Jelena's synthetic 5-layer networks (`networks/multilayer/Jelena/network_incomplete/`, 0% and 50% of links removed) are not in the fetch manifest yet.
- `-N10` and `-2 -N10` run once; `-N1` is the interleaved minimum of 3. The old arm crashed on aucs `-N10` in this session (#1375, below); that row's old cells come from a re-run that completed, and match the first pass to every digit. The run happened under the same load as the re-timing, so instructions carry the cost comparison; seconds are shown but are not evidence.

What it shows:
- **The old engine's numbers were not the objective.** Wherever the prior applies, its partitions score 1.0% to 475% longer under it than it reported (multilayer_intra_inter has no intra-layer prior to record): DanioRerio `-N10` reported 1.770 bits for a partition that scores 10.175, and EU airlines 4.785 for one that scores 8.775.
- **The new engine matches OO** to every printed digit on the two toys and EU airlines. It is shorter than OO on malaria (−0.235% at `-N10`), StarWars (−0.087%; −1.20% at `-N1`), DanioRerio (−0.044%), aucs (`-2` −0.035%, `-N1` −0.463%) and Jelena 50% (−0.001%; −0.012% at `-N1`). It is longer than OO on two cells: aucs `-N10` +0.082% and Jelena 0% `-N10`/`-2 -N10` +0.010%, both below 0.1%.
- **Cost against the old engine:** it does less work on most rows, for example malaria −2.9% to −9.0% in instructions, EU airlines −10% to −45%, DanioRerio `-N10` −30% and Jelena 50% `-N10` −33%. It does more only on single-trial rows where it also finds a far shorter partition: Jelena 0% `-N1` +32% for −5.0% in bits, Jelena 50% `-N1` +15% for −16.3%, aucs `-N1` +12% for −7.8%.
- **The old arm crashes on aucs `-N10` on 6 to 8 of 10 runs** (SIGTRAP in malloc). The cause is #1375: `splitLevelModules` seeded its piece-level move loop with uncompacted module ids and wrote past its arrays. It predates this PR and #1373. The new arm does not reach that path on aucs, and AddressSanitizer is clean on 54 runs of this PR with the #1375 fix (the identity set plus every network here at `-N10`, `-2` and `-d`). The fix gets its own sub-PR.
- **Cost against OO at `-N10`:** −45% in instructions on malaria, −39% on aucs, −51% on StarWars, −24% on DanioRerio and −33% on both Jelena networks. It is more only on EU airlines (+76%) and the first toy, all runs of a few milliseconds.
