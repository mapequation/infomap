## Performance

> Manual old-vs-new benchmark of the `--columnar` engine over the set in [`columnar_wip/benchmark-networks.md`](columnar_wip/benchmark-networks.md). This is **not** the CI `perf-pr.yml` check, which only sees the default OO path since the new core is flag-gated.

Single-threaded (`MODE=release OPENMP=0`), `--seed 123`. Codelength in bits. **`instr` is instructions retired** (`/usr/bin/time -l`). `time` is `--timing-json`'s `timing.total_s`.
- `-N10` rows run once (deterministic; `instr` carries the comparison).
- `-N1` rows are the interleaved minimum of 3.
- Driver: [`columnar_wip/bench-sync.py`](columnar_wip/bench-sync.py) `--carry-oo`, writing [`columnar_wip/parent-exit-ab-results.tsv`](columnar_wip/parent-exit-ab-results.tsv), old and new on every row of the previous snapshot (#1377).
- Changed configurations, re-timed: [`columnar_wip/retime-changed.py`](columnar_wip/retime-changed.py), every configuration whose codelength changes as the minimum of 5, writing [`columnar_wip/parent-exit-retime.tsv`](columnar_wip/parent-exit-retime.tsv).
- Outliers in seconds: [`columnar_wip/retime-outliers.py`](columnar_wip/retime-outliers.py), writing [`columnar_wip/parent-exit-outliers-retime.tsv`](columnar_wip/parent-exit-outliers-retime.tsv).
- Per kind of site: `bench-sync.py` on the changed rows with a build that changes only the nested-level sites and one that changes only the proposal sites, writing [`columnar_wip/parent-exit-nested-only.tsv`](columnar_wip/parent-exit-nested-only.tsv) and [`columnar_wip/parent-exit-proposal-only.tsv`](columnar_wip/parent-exit-proposal-only.tsv) (an earlier session the same day, driven from [`columnar_wip/parent-exit-rows.tsv`](columnar_wip/parent-exit-rows.tsv)).
- Regularized multilayer (feature build): [`columnar_wip/bench-multilayer.py`](columnar_wip/bench-multilayer.py), writing [`columnar_wip/parent-exit-ml-results.tsv`](columnar_wip/parent-exit-ml-results.tsv).
- Tables: [`columnar_wip/assemble-hier-gaps.py`](columnar_wip/assemble-hier-gaps.py) builds every table from the TSV that [`columnar_wip/tables-1042.py`](columnar_wip/tables-1042.py) `assembly` writes, including the OO cells. The REG table comes from `tables-1042.py reg`.

> **This PR gives the columnar sub-searches their parent module's total exit under recorded teleportation (#1372).** Under `--regularized` and `--recorded-teleportation` a module's exit is its link exit plus its teleport exit. Five sub-searches that re-cluster inside a parent were handed the parent's exit with the teleport part subtracted (`ColumnarBoundaryFlow::linkExit`), so they priced leaving the parent too cheaply. They now get the stored total exit, and `linkExit` is gone. The finding is F76.
>
> The five sites are of two kinds:
> - **Nested-level sites:** `refineLayerWithinGrandparent`, which is gated on the true codelength, and `refineBottomWithinParents`, which is not. Their sub-search becomes a real level of the hierarchy, whose codelength codes the parent's total exit.
> - **Proposal sites:** `splitTopModules` and `splitLevelModules` (two sites). The sub-search only proposes pieces, which the recombination keeps only if the true codelength improves. Here the exit is a heuristic.
>
> What the snapshot shows:
> - **Only runs that record teleportation change.** 33 of those 121 configurations change and 88 are bit-identical. The 211 configurations without teleportation are all bit-identical, with instructions at a median of −0.02%. Seconds summed over the set: −0.41%.
> - **Normal networks.** wikispeedia gets shorter in all 8 configurations that change:
>   - `-d --regularized -N1`: −0.254% in bits, at +0.45% in instructions;
>   - `--regularized -N10`: −0.046% in bits, at +3.0% in instructions, or +8.35% with `-d`;
>   - `--recorded-teleportation`: −0.002% to −0.004% in bits, with instructions within ±0.2%.
>
>   Re-timed as the minimum of 5, its seconds move by −3.8% to +5.5%. web-NotreDame `-d --recorded-teleportation` gets longer: +0.045% in bits, at −3.3% (`-N10`) and −6.6% (`-N1`) in instructions, and −3.6% and −3.9% in seconds.
> - **Where the normal-network changes come from.** Three changes come from `refineLayerWithinGrandparent`, and `refineBottomWithinParents` moves none of the configurations it was checked on:
>   - web-NotreDame's +0.045% in bits;
>   - wikispeedia `-N1`'s −0.254% in bits;
>   - about 7 points of wikispeedia `-d --regularized -N10`'s +8.35% in instructions, for no change in bits. They are spent in deep repair, on gated sub-searches whose results are rejected.
>
>   wikispeedia's −0.046% at `-N10` comes from the proposal sites.
> - **The om family:** 23 configurations change.
>   - **Shorter:** om3 −0.019%, om4 E100k `-N10` −0.009%, om5 −0.003%, om2 E100k −0.002% or less.
>   - **Longer:** om2 E50k `-2d -N10` +0.095% (at −7.3% in instructions), om4 E100k `-N1` +0.013% (at −1.6%), om5 E100k `-2d -N10` +0.008%, om3 E100k `-N1` +0.0008%.
>   - **More work at the same codelength:** om2 E50k `-N1` +13% to +14% in instructions (+11% to +15% in seconds), and om2 E50k `-d -N10` +5.6%. Its two-level deep repair needs 9 fresh derivations instead of 6 to reach the same codelength (F76). This was accepted for this issue.
>   - **Bit-identical but more work:** om8 E50k `--regularized -N1` takes +3.6% to +4.1% in instructions.
> - **Regularized multilayer (feature build):** 5 of 27 configurations change: aucs `-N1` −0.132% in bits, malaria −0.060% (`-N1`) and −0.009% (`-N10`, `-2 -N10`), and Jelena synthetic 50% `-N1` +0.016%. Instructions on those 5 move by −3.2% to +0.5%.
> - **The slow cells are noise.** The 35 unchanged configurations more than 10% slower in the sweep, re-run alternating as the minimum of 5: instructions −0.79% to +0.06%, seconds −5.0% to +24.5%. The largest is a 0.44 s planted-partition row.
>
> Columnar arms measured in one session, 2026-10-23. OO arms carried from #1377, which carried them from #1376, #1373 and #1369's session.
> - **Old:** a fresh build of the branch tip `fd41270c` (#1377), md5 `77eec5e8c6ba2277cc83375dba0b76ee`. It is byte-identical to #1377's new arm. Feature build: md5 `f94bd19808f2fadf60dd68d4fcb0dcca`, also identical.
> - **New:** `95024dd8`, md5 `64f033ed29985cdbd96d90ea836e5c06`. Feature build: md5 `fe6c5918cb4cc916ae014cf55ff47d97`.
> - **Family rows** that repeat a `-C`, `-C -2` or `-C -N1` row take that row's run, so the two tables agree.
