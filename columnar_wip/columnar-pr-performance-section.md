## Performance

> Manual old-vs-new benchmark of the `--columnar` engine over the set in [`columnar_wip/benchmark-networks.md`](columnar_wip/benchmark-networks.md). This is **not** the CI `perf-pr.yml` check, which only sees the default OO path since the new core is flag-gated.

Single-threaded (`MODE=release OPENMP=0`), `--seed 123`. Codelength in bits. **`instr` is instructions retired** (`/usr/bin/time -l`). `time` is `--timing-json`'s `timing.total_s`.
- `-N10` rows run once (deterministic; `instr` carries the comparison).
- `-N1` rows are the interleaved minimum of 3.
- Driver: [`columnar_wip/bench-sync.py`](columnar_wip/bench-sync.py) with `--carry-oo`, writing [`columnar_wip/console-ab-results.tsv`](columnar_wip/console-ab-results.tsv). One session, old and new on every columnar row of the #1168 snapshot. The OO rows are carried from that snapshot's session.
- Tables: [`columnar_wip/assemble-hier-gaps.py`](columnar_wip/assemble-hier-gaps.py) builds every table from the TSV that [`columnar_wip/tables-1042.py`](columnar_wip/tables-1042.py) `assembly` writes, now including the OO cells. The REG table comes from `tables-1042.py reg`.

> **This PR changes only what the columnar engine prints**: the trial's status lines, and a section of its own for the passes that run after the trials. It computes nothing new, so this snapshot's job is to show that nothing moved.
> - **All 332 columnar configurations are bit-identical**, old against new, in codelength, top modules and levels: `-C`, `-C -2`, `-C -N1`, `-F`, `--non-redundant`, the overlapping family and the REG rows.
> - **Instructions do not move.** Over the 295 configurations above 0.3G instructions, the median change is −0.009% (5th–95th percentile −0.20% to +0.16%). The largest is netscience REG `--regularized -N10`, −1.9% at 0.32G.
> - **Seconds moved with the load, not the code.** The single `-N10` runs spread from −12.6% to +19.1% (5th–95th percentile; median +0.59%) at flat instructions. Re-run interleaved, as the minimum of 5 per arm, the eight slowest of them land between −4.8% and +2.1% (see [The slow cells are load](#the-slow-cells-are-load)).
> - **OO is carried, not measured.** This PR changes nothing the OO engine computes, so every OO cell is the #1168 session's new arm, which is the OO engine at this branch's tip. The OO-vs-columnar seconds therefore compare two sessions.
>
> Measured in one session, 2026-10-19.
> - **Old:** a fresh build of the branch tip `f12fcfc1` (#1168), md5 `8ee83b9936fe3c20a54754724f56b87e`. This is byte-identical to #1168's new arm.
> - **New:** this PR at `ab3d0083`, md5 `3eccc7b2d4e7a3118c84b3534116d98a`.
> - **Load:** the 1-minute load ran from 8 to 29, with interactive applications busy throughout and no other benchmark running. The arms are interleaved per row. `-N1` rows are the minimum of 3 reps spread across the batch.
> - **Family rows** that repeat a `-C`, `-C -2` or `-C -N1` row take that row's run, so the two tables agree.
