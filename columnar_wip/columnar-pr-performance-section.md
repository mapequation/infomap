> **This PR makes `--preferred-number-of-modules` count the top modules in the columnar engine (#1068).** The bias |K − K_pref| counted leaf modules; OO counts the root's children, and #1068 settled on the top modules, the ones a user asking for K modules is handed. The penalty alone is not enough: the tip's search steers the leaf count, and its trees, priced at the top, leave 5 or 6 top modules and pay a bit for each one short (jazz K = 100 at 103.1 bits, word_assoc K = 3000 at 3005.7). So the search builds the top for the bias:
> - **The bottom and the up-build run unbiased.** The bias enters only where top modules are formed. When the unbiased bottom has fewer modules than K_pref, the top is the leaf partition, searched with the bias from that bottom and from singletons. When the up-build would drop below K_pref, the modules of the last level above it are regrouped with the bias, from singletons and from the last unbiased super partition, and the regroup is kept only if it beats that level plus its penalty. The top-layer refine prices its regroup with the bias too.
> - **A level split drops the modules it empties** (`ColumnarStack::reaggregateFrom`). A relocation across grandparents could leave a module without children. That costs nothing in the map equation, but a level's size stopped being its module count, and the penalty read it as one: air30k K = 20 reported 5.4280 bits for a tree with 13 top modules that scores 12.4280. The #1375 seed workaround, which existed for the same empty ids, goes.
> - **The dissolve runs under the bias, below the top modules.** It was off, as in OO. Dissolving a module below the top leaves the count alone, so the pass now only keeps the top modules out of its candidates. Its stamps carry the penalty on the root; without that the per-level table summed to the penalty less (netscience K = 50: 3.36 bits under a reported 221.36).
>
> [Per-part attribution](#per-part-attribution) is below; the finding is F83. The top it builds can be **padded**: where no natural level lies near K_pref, the cheapest K top modules are one module holding nearly everything plus small ones (word_assoc K = 25: 1.0 effective top modules, OO 21.4). That is the objective's optimum; OO avoids it only because it builds its top as a flat partition first. Kept as is, to keep the code small for a rarely used option.
>

#### Every cell that is worse, explained

- **In bits, against the old columnar:** none. The 4 changed configurations are lower. On the 24 runs this PR is never higher than the tip's tree priced at the top; jazz K = 10 ties.
- **In bits, against OO on the 24 runs:**
  - word_assoc K = 3000, +0.80% in both searches. The result is the `-2` partition, unchanged by this PR. #1406's deep repair for the bias, still off, reaches −2.0% there.
  - netscience is the component floor (#1407).
  - air30k is no comparison: OO optimizes without the bias.
  - In `-2`, unchanged: polblogs K = 5 +0.12%, science2001 K = 25 +0.22%.
- **In seconds, on changed rows: all 4, +14.0% to +68.3%,** for −1.75% to −5.19% in bits. That is 9 to 17 times as much in seconds as in bits, and 18 to 25 times in instructions.
  - **Where it goes.** A profile of word_assoc K = 25 `-C -N10` (`sample`, 10 s) puts it in the refine of the deeper tree. `refineLayerWithinGrandparent` grows from 248 to 1264 samples, the build with its regroup from 288 to 555, and the coarsening shrinks from 696 to 71.
  - **Why.** The tip's answer there is flat. This PR's has a level under the 25 top modules, one of which holds nearly all of the network, so re-deriving the layer within each grandparent is close to a whole-network re-solve.
  - **What it buys.** A `COLUMNAR_DEBUG` build (throwaway) shows that refine is the gain: on `-N1` the built tree is 11.7245 bits and the refined one 11.4585.
  - **This is a trade, and it is not taken silently: it needs Daniel's decision.**
- **On the 24 runs, against the tip, the standard search is more than 1% slower on 10 of the 12** (air30k +0.6%, powergrid K = 1500 +0.4%). Each is lower in bits or tied:
  - **word_assoc K = 100: +17.4% in seconds (+33.7% in instructions) for −0.16% in bits, about 200 times as much.** This is the marginal one, the same padded refine as K = 25.
  - The leaf-crossing rows (jazz K = 10 and 100, polblogs K = 400): +12.5% to +13.7%, under 0.07 s, at +7.7% to +15.0% in instructions.
  - netscience +21.6% (0.006 s), polblogs K = 5 +11.4%, powergrid K = 30 +7.9%, science2001 +7.8%, word_assoc K = 3000 +4.7%.
- **In seconds, on bit-identical rows:** none after re-timing. The largest re-timed outliers read −2.1% to +0.6% at the minimum (air30k `-C -N10`: +22.3% in the sweep, −0.8% re-timed, at +0.12% in instructions). The ones that stay positive are within noise: multilayer (ex.) `-C -N1` (+12%, a sub-millisecond run), om3_N256_E50k `-2d --regularized -N10` +0.6%, wikispeedia `-C -F -d -N10` +0.3%.
