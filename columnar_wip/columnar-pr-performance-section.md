## Performance

> Manual old-vs-new benchmark of the `--columnar` engine over the set in [`columnar_wip/benchmark-networks.md`](columnar_wip/benchmark-networks.md). This is **not** the CI `perf-pr.yml` check, which only sees the default OO path since the new core is flag-gated.

Single-threaded (`MODE=release OPENMP=0`), `--seed 123`. Codelength in bits. **`instr` is instructions retired** (`/usr/bin/time -l`). `time` is `--timing-json`'s `timing.total_s`.
- `-N10` rows run once (deterministic; `instr` carries the comparison).
- `-N1` rows are the interleaved minimum of 3.
- Driver: [`columnar_wip/bench-sync.py`](columnar_wip/bench-sync.py), writing [`columnar_wip/sync-1365-ab-results.tsv`](columnar_wip/sync-1365-ab-results.tsv), old and new on every row of the #1358 snapshot. The columnar arms ran in one session; the OO rows were seeded into the TSV from this sync's first session, both arms, marked carried.
- Tables: [`columnar_wip/assemble-hier-gaps.py`](columnar_wip/assemble-hier-gaps.py) builds every table from the TSV that [`columnar_wip/tables-1042.py`](columnar_wip/tables-1042.py) `assembly` writes, including the OO cells. The REG table comes from `tables-1042.py reg`.

> **This PR syncs master into `columnar-hierarchical-core`: 111 commits, #1163 through #1365, as merge `04bae660`.** Master fixed the flow in code this branch had retired or reshaped (`InfomapBase::initEnterExitFlow()` and the InfomapBase variable-Markov-time block). The merge brings each fix in the branch's own form, as F72 records:
> - the columnar teleport strip and module pricing take `recordsTeleportation()` (#1194, #1196);
> - the columnar leaf takes master's link-based regularized enter/exit (#1189);
> - regularized multilayer enter/exit adds the scaled links in `finalize()`, without self-links (#1317, #1287);
> - variable Markov time gets master's per-link scale, floors and pair count (#1264, #1191, #1334, #1203);
> - both engines share one entropy-correction divisor (#1203).
>
> Two fixes follow the merge:
> - **Markov time (`d3b50048`).** The branch stored the Markov-scaled link flow in the network, which is the flow the output reports. Master's new oracles (#1177, #1225) require the flow of Markov time 1 there, and they failed on this PR's CI. The network now keeps that flow, and both engines read a Markov-scaled copy, so no codelength moves.
> - **#1368 (`f8536bd9`).** Under `--columnar` the memory objective aggregates physical nodes for the leaf modules only, as before master's #1241: only the OO hierarchical search reads the other modules' aggregates, and the columnar engine never runs it.
>
> `6f85b6bf` then merges #1370 from master, which lets a sync pass the reference-edit CI gate. It changes no `src/`.
>
> What the snapshot shows:
> - **Columnar: 310 of 332 configurations are bit-identical** in codelength, top modules and levels. Over the 277 of them above 0.3G instructions, the median instruction change is −0.002% (5th–95th percentile −0.70% to +0.47%).
> - **22 columnar configurations change, all under `--regularized`:**
>   - 15 because master changed the regularized objective; the old partition scores differently under the new binary. Ten land on exactly the old partition. lazega's +2.57% to +2.79% in bits is #1214's pair count, and OO moves identically.
>   - 7 because the columnar leaf input became exact (wikispeedia and air30k `-d`/`-2d --regularized`; same objective): −0.19% to +0.17% in bits at seed 123. At `-N10`, 9 of 10 other seeds are shorter.
> - **The other fixes have no row in the set.** No configuration uses variable Markov time, recorded teleportation without teleportation, or regularized multilayer input. Their evidence is F72 and the new tests.
> - **OO: 10 of 28 configurations change, all longer and faster, from two master commits.**
>   - #1282 (sub-searches code the module exit on memory networks): wikispeedia, malaria, air30k and air30k (reg.), +0.05% to +0.46% in bits at −5% to −53% in seconds.
>   - #1292 (`--preferred-number-of-modules` kept out of sub-searches): word_assoc (pref.), +0.28% in bits at −55% to −60% in seconds.
>   - Master builds just before and at each commit reproduce the old and new values exactly.
>   - The 18 unchanged OO configurations cost a median +2.3% in instructions, also master's: its own base and tip builds reproduce it (word_assoc 54.2G → 55.0G).
> - **Seconds:** summed over the 332 columnar configurations, −0.92% in the sweep and −1.64% with its 42 outliers re-timed interleaved (minimum of 5). The re-timed unchanged columnar rows land between −9.4% and +3.5%, at −0.87% to +0.60% in instructions. One real cost remains: the changed wikispeedia `--regularized` rows, +1.1% to +5.3% in seconds on their new trajectories.
> - **Master's #1241 cost on deep memory-network trees is gone (#1368).** om5–om8 E50k `-d` is at −0.22% to +0.16% in instructions against the tip, from +1.3% to +2.4% with the merge alone.
>
> Columnar arms measured in one session, 2026-10-22. OO arms carried from this sync's first session, 2026-10-21.
> - **Old:** a fresh build of the branch tip `13c27f95` (#1358), md5 `ba3bdd9274d77e580529564c99541c64`. It is byte-identical to #1358's new arm.
> - **New:** `f8536bd9`, md5 `7b8bdfad0152ddf4f1f30a9560b8e5c5`, built clean from HEAD. The master merge `6f85b6bf` after it builds to the same md5. The first session's new arm was the merge `04bae660` (md5 `3069d054`), and all 332 columnar results are identical between the two sessions, on both arms.
> - **OO arms** are carried from the first session, old against the merge `04bae660`, because neither fix changes what the OO engine computes or costs: the Markov-time fix keeps the coded flow bit-identical, and the #1368 fix applies under `--columnar` only. air30k (meta) is not run (#1134).
> - **Load:** no other benchmark or test suite ran during the sweep. The arms are interleaved per row. `-N1` rows are the minimum of 3 reps spread across the batch.
> - **Family rows** that repeat a `-C`, `-C -2` or `-C -N1` row take that row's run, so the two tables agree.

**Master's #1241 cost on deep memory-network trees is fixed here (#1368).** In this sync's first session the om5–om8 E50k `-d` rows were bit-identical but +1.3% to +2.4% in instructions, at `-N1` and `-N10` alike.
- A profile of om8_N256_E50k `-d -N10` put it in `MemMapEquation::initPhysicalNodes`: 0.68% of samples on old, 2.05% on the merge.
- #1241 (#1209) makes the OO memory objective aggregate physical nodes for every module, because the OO hierarchical search turns modules of modules into leaf modules (`removeSubModules`) and reads them. The columnar engine reached that code through `initTree`, which materializes each trial's result into the InfoNode tree, but it never runs that search.
- Under `--columnar` the objective now aggregates the leaf modules only, as before #1241. The same rows are at −0.22% to +0.16% in instructions against the tip, output bit-identical.

### Every benchmark network under recorded teleportation

Every non-family network of the benchmark set under `--regularized`, and also under
`--recorded-teleportation` when it is directed, as #1163 added them. air30k `-d --regularized` is the
tables' "air30k (reg.)". multilayer and malaria are left out, because `--regularized` on multilayer input
needs the regularized-multilayer feature build. Rows run `-N10` once and `-N1` as the interleaved minimum
of 3.

21 rows change, all under `--regularized`. Every `--recorded-teleportation` row is bit-identical, and so are
netscience, powergrid, jazz, ninetriangles and web-NotreDame. Scoring the old binary's partition with both
binaries (`--no-infomap -c`) separates two causes:
- **Master changed the objective on 15 rows** (air30k, air30k (meta), lazega, polblogs, word_assoc): the old
  partition scores differently under the new binary.
  - Ten of them land on exactly the old partition, so only the number moves: +0.000001% to +0.0012% in bits.
  - lazega is #1214. All 399 of its pairs are listed both ways, and the regularized prior now counts each
    pair once: +2.57% to +2.79% in bits. OO moves identically (6.280773357 → 6.455952473 at `-N10`, both
    engines). On the new objective the new search is 0.03% shorter than the old partition.
  - air30k (meta) `--regularized -N1` (+0.078%) and air30k `--regularized -N1` (+0.016%) end slightly above
    the old partition on the new objective.
- **The search's input changed on the six wikispeedia rows**; their objective is the same. The columnar leaf
  now takes master's link-based regularized enter/exit (#1189), exact where the tip's node-flow shortcut was
  off by 1.4e-4 on wikispeedia. At seed 123: −0.15% in bits (`-d -N10`), +0.045% (the three `-2d -N10`
  rows), −0.006% and +0.17% at `-N1`. Other seeds:
  - `-d -N10`: 4 of 5 shorter, mean −0.049%.
  - `-2d -N10`: all 5 shorter, mean −0.034%.
  - `-d -N1`, 20 seeds: median +0.012%, from −0.41% to +0.41%, 7 shorter and 10 longer.
