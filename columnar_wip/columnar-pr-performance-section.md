## Performance

> Manual old-vs-new benchmark of the `--columnar` engine over the set in [`columnar_wip/benchmark-networks.md`](columnar_wip/benchmark-networks.md). This is **not** the CI `perf-pr.yml` check, which only sees the default OO path since the new core is flag-gated.

Single-threaded (`MODE=release OPENMP=0`), `--seed 123`. Codelength in bits. **`instr` is instructions retired** (`/usr/bin/time -l`). `time` is `--timing-json`'s `timing.total_s`.
- `-N10` rows run once (deterministic; `instr` carries the comparison).
- `-N1` rows are the interleaved minimum of 3.
- Driver: [`columnar_wip/bench-1140.py`](columnar_wip/bench-1140.py), writing [`columnar_wip/1140-ab-results.tsv`](columnar_wip/1140-ab-results.tsv).
- The tables are built from that TSV by [`columnar_wip/assemble-hier-gaps.py`](columnar_wip/assemble-hier-gaps.py).

> **This PR changes when the objective-option warnings fire, and nothing the engine computes (F66).**
> - `--entropy-corrected` and `--preferred-number-of-modules` warned "has no effect on this run" under `-C` on state, multilayer and meta-data input, while the columnar engine applied both: states.net `-C` went from 2.011405238 to 2.169200009 bits under `--entropy-corrected`, next to a warning that it reported the uncorrected codelength.
> - The check now asks the engine that runs. The one `-C` run that is scored by the object-oriented objective, `--no-infomap` without an input partition, still warns, and only about the entropy term.
> - Both checks run once per run, before the trials, and touch nothing the search or the scoring reads. So this snapshot is here to show that nothing moves.

> **Old** = a fresh `MODE=release OPENMP=0` build of `columnar-hierarchical-core` tip `501f523a`, md5 `eddfdee90cfa3f43227d53b848af8821`. **New** = this PR at `78d58569`, md5 `acb26dc549cf3f155b8dbd00f29c0d88`, built clean the same way.
> - **One session (2026-10-11)**, every configuration of the #1136 snapshot, both arms, interleaved per row with the first arm alternating. `-N1` rows are the minimum of 3 reps spread across the batch, and `-N10` rows run once. Load spikes from other work on the machine inflated some cells by up to 7.9× in seconds, so two more rounds re-measured both arms of every cell the load had distorted: the 22 where new first read more than 3% slower (3 extra reps, and 4 more for the two still above +3%), and 18 that ran more than 15% slower than #1136's time for the same source or read more than 5% faster (5 extra reps). A cell is the minimum over its reps, 1 033 runs in all.
> - **A different machine from the previous snapshots, with the same source:** `501f523a` has the `src/` of `c9ce210b`, #1136's binary. Its old column reproduces #1136's new column in bits, top modules and levels on all 177 paired configurations, and runs at 0.991× #1136's seconds (median over the 341 cells of at least 0.05 s, quartiles 0.96–1.02). The two machines are as fast as each other.
> - **Load was 2.4–165.5** (the 1-minute average at each run's start, median 7.8) from other work on the machine, so read `instr` before `time`.
> - **OO arms:** the OO engine is untouched, so its cells are carried from the #1136 snapshot. Those are the #1079-day session's, except the rows #1136 re-measured on the new inputs, all on the previous snapshots' machine. This machine runs the same source as fast (above), so the OO-vs-columnar seconds still compare.
>   - OO `air30k (meta)` `-N1` on seed 123 still has no number: it ran for 9 h 25 min and was killed (#1134).

### What the change moves

Nothing. All 177 paired configurations are bit-identical between the arms in bits, top modules and levels, on every one of the 1 033 runs, so the table below has no rows.
- **Instructions:** −2.53% to +0.60% (median −0.03%). Every cell beyond ±0.3% is a row under 0.07 s, where process start-up is a large share of the count: multilayer, ninetriangles, netscience, jazz and lazega. One arm's reps spread about as much on a long row: the eight old runs of om3 E50000 `-d -N10` span 42.66–42.74 G.
- **Seconds:** −29.0% to +6.0% (median −0.36%). 41 cells read more than 1% slower and 57 more than 1% faster (below).

**Every cell where new is worse than old, and why.**

No cell is worse in bits. In seconds, 41 of the 177 paired cells read more than 1% slower, at −0.96% to +0.30% in instructions, and 57 read more than 1% faster. Nothing in the change runs during the search: it adds two checks of a few comparisons each, once per run, before the trials. The two largest:
- **om3 E50000 `-d`, `-C -N10`: +6.0%** in seconds as the minimum of 8 reps per arm, at +0.03% in instructions. One old rep took 4.16 s, 6% below every other run of either arm (4.42–4.63 s). The medians are 4.50 s (old) and 4.49 s (new).
- **om5 planted, `-2d --no-infomap -c`: +2.9%** in seconds as the minimum of 10, at +0.13% in instructions, on a 0.12 s row whose reps spread about ±5% in each arm.

The other 39 are at +1.0% to +2.8% in seconds, three of them under 10 ms.
