## Performance

> Manual old-vs-new benchmark of the `--columnar` engine over the set in [`columnar_wip/benchmark-networks.md`](columnar_wip/benchmark-networks.md). This is **not** the CI `perf-pr.yml` check, which only sees the default OO path since the new core is flag-gated.

Single-threaded (`MODE=release OPENMP=0`), `--seed 123`. Codelength in bits. **`instr` is instructions retired** (`/usr/bin/time -l`). `time` is `--timing-json`'s `timing.total_s`.
- `-N10` rows run once (deterministic; `instr` carries the comparison).
- `-N1` rows are the interleaved minimum of 3.
- Driver: [`columnar_wip/bench-sync.py`](columnar_wip/bench-sync.py) `--carry-oo`, writing [`columnar_wip/pg-accept-ab-results.tsv`](columnar_wip/pg-accept-ab-results.tsv), old and new on every row of the previous snapshot (#1380).
- Re-timed: every configuration whose codelength changed, with [`columnar_wip/retime-changed.py`](columnar_wip/retime-changed.py), writing [`columnar_wip/pg-accept-retime-changed.tsv`](columnar_wip/pg-accept-retime-changed.tsv). The outliers in seconds, with [`columnar_wip/retime-outliers.py`](columnar_wip/retime-outliers.py), writing [`columnar_wip/pg-accept-retime.tsv`](columnar_wip/pg-accept-retime.tsv).
- Tables: [`columnar_wip/assemble-hier-gaps.py`](columnar_wip/assemble-hier-gaps.py) builds every table from the TSV that [`columnar_wip/tables-1042.py`](columnar_wip/tables-1042.py) `assembly` writes, including the OO cells. The REG table comes from `tables-1042.py reg`.

> **This PR keeps or undoes each grandparent separately in the columnar refine (#1387).** `refineLayerWithinGrandparent` re-partitions layer k inside every grandparent and used to keep or reject the whole new layer in one gated step. A grandparent's enter and exit do not change with its insides, so the layer's codelength change is the sum of per-grandparent changes. The refine reads them from the stack's breakdown before and after the new layer, and undoes only the grandparents that got worse.
>
> It comes with three parts:
> - a content version for `ColumnarStack`, so the refine's sweeps score each stack once;
> - corrections that read only the leaf partition are reused while it holds;
> - re-sweeps skip the grandparents the previous re-sweep left untouched.
>
> [Per-part attribution](#per-part-attribution) is below; the finding is F79.
>
> What the snapshot shows:
> - **Codelength changes on 47 of 332 rows: 33 lower (mean −0.144% in bits), 14 higher (mean +0.050%).** The gains are the overlapping family at 50k links (−0.12% to −0.29%) and L\* on powergrid (−0.82%) and netscience (−0.27%). The 5 more seeds in [the seed check](#five-more-seeds) hold all of them at every seed.
> - **Three rows are more than 0.1% worse in bits at seed 123.**

>   - `-C -N1` air30k (meta): +0.105%, mean +0.065% over 6 seeds, worse at 2.
> - **Instructions: median 0.00%, 5th–95th percentile −1.79% to +2.66%, summed −0.29%.** Seconds summed −0.32% in the sweep, and −1.35% over the 47 changed configurations re-timed as the minimum of 5.
