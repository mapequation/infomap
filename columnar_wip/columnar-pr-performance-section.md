## Performance

> Manual old-vs-new benchmark of the `--columnar` engine over the set in [`columnar_wip/benchmark-networks.md`](columnar_wip/benchmark-networks.md). This is **not** the CI `perf-pr.yml` check, which only sees the default OO path since the new core is flag-gated.

Single-threaded (`MODE=release OPENMP=0`), `--seed 123`. Codelength in bits. **`instr` is instructions retired** (`/usr/bin/time -l`). `time` is `--timing-json`'s `timing.total_s`.
- `-N10` rows run once (deterministic; `instr` carries the comparison).
- `-N1` rows are the interleaved minimum of 3.
- Driver: [`columnar_wip/bench-sync.py`](columnar_wip/bench-sync.py) `--carry-oo`, writing [`columnar_wip/pref-mm-ab-results.tsv`](columnar_wip/pref-mm-ab-results.tsv), old and new on every row of the #1401 snapshot.
- Re-timed: every configuration whose codelength changed, with [`columnar_wip/retime-changed.py`](columnar_wip/retime-changed.py), writing [`columnar_wip/pref-mm-retime-changed.tsv`](columnar_wip/pref-mm-retime-changed.tsv). The outliers in seconds, with [`columnar_wip/retime-outliers.py`](columnar_wip/retime-outliers.py), writing [`columnar_wip/pref-mm-retime.tsv`](columnar_wip/pref-mm-retime.tsv).
- Per-pass attribution: 24 preferred-modules runs, final codelength, writing [`columnar_wip/pref-mm-attribution.tsv`](columnar_wip/pref-mm-attribution.tsv) ([below](#per-part-attribution)).
- Feature build: not re-run (#1401's [`columnar_wip/refine-skip-feature.tsv`](columnar_wip/refine-skip-feature.tsv), [below](#the-feature-build)).
- Tables: [`columnar_wip/assemble-hier-gaps.py`](columnar_wip/assemble-hier-gaps.py) builds every table from the TSV that [`columnar_wip/tables-1042.py`](columnar_wip/tables-1042.py) `assembly` writes, including the OO cells. The REG table comes from `tables-1042.py reg`.

> **This PR puts the preferred-modules bias in the columnar aggregation passes.** `PreferredModulesCorrection` (`--preferred-number-of-modules`) was in the leaf move loop but not in the module passes. So the aggregation optimized the base objective and stopped at the natural module count (about 760 on word_assoc). `optimizeTwoLevelStack`'s gated merges then dragged K down to K_pref one greedy merge at a time: a third of the trial, and 0.62% in bits above OO on word_assoc (pref.) `-2`. It has two parts:
> - **The bias joins the module passes** (`participatesInModuleMoves`), as the memory and metadata corrections did in #834. Its state is a member count per module, so K is counted the same whether the movers are leaves or units. Joining also switches on the descending trajectory repair (#889), which pays: without it 10 of the 24 runs below end higher in bits, by up to 0.55%, and one ends 0.05% lower.
> - **A new correction trait, `tracksUnitAttributes()` (memory and metadata), keeps three passes off for the bias, as on the tip:** the regroup ladder, the leaf-level split and the winner's deep repair. They were gated on joining the module passes, and they exist for an attribute codebook's reward, which grows faster than module size; a module-count bias has no such reward. `hasModuleMoveCorrections` becomes `hasAttributeCorrections`.
>
> [Per-part attribution](#per-part-attribution) is below; the finding is F82. **One question stays open:** the deep repair does lower the bits on some preferred-modules runs, by up to 2.0%, at +24% in instructions summed over the two-level runs. This PR leaves it off, as on the tip; [the trade](#the-deep-repair-for-the-bias-an-open-trade) is below.
>
> What the snapshot shows:
> - **Codelength changes on 4 of the 332 configurations, all word_assoc (pref.), all lower in bits:** `-C -2` −0.81%, `-C` and `-C -F` −0.76%, L\* −1.14%. Every other configuration is bit-identical. That includes word_assoc (pref.) `-C -N1`, whose single hierarchical-first trial never runs the two-level aggregation.
> - **The 4 changed configurations are 13.5% to 26.8% faster in seconds**, re-timed as the minimum of 5 (−17.2% summed), at −13.2% to −24.6% in instructions.
> - **The bit-identical configurations do not move in instructions:** +0.003% summed, median −0.004% over the 291 above 0.3G (5th–95th percentile −0.17% to +0.06%). In seconds they sum to +0.28% in the sweep; the one outlier left after re-timing is explained [below](#every-cell-that-is-worse-explained).
> - **Single trials:** the only `-N1` row the change can reach, word_assoc (pref.) `-C -N1`, is bit-identical at +0.02% in instructions and +0.8% in seconds.
> - **Against OO, word_assoc (pref.) ends below it in bits:** `-2` −0.196% at −14.8% in instructions (it was +0.62% and +13%), standard −0.152% at −21.2%.
>
