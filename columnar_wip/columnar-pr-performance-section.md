## Performance

> Manual old-vs-new benchmark of the `--columnar` engine over the set in [`columnar_wip/benchmark-networks.md`](columnar_wip/benchmark-networks.md). This is **not** the CI `perf-pr.yml` check, which only sees the default OO path since the new core is flag-gated.

Single-threaded (`MODE=release OPENMP=0`), `--seed 123`. Codelength in bits. **`instr` is instructions retired** (`/usr/bin/time -l`). `time` is `--timing-json`'s `timing.total_s`.
- `-N10` rows run once (deterministic; `instr` carries the comparison).
- `-N1` rows are the interleaved minimum of 3.
- Driver: [`columnar_wip/bench-sync.py`](columnar_wip/bench-sync.py) with `--carry-oo`, writing [`columnar_wip/merge-tele-ab-results.tsv`](columnar_wip/merge-tele-ab-results.tsv). One session, old and new on every columnar row of the #1351 snapshot. The OO rows are carried from #1351, which carried them from the #1168 session.
- Tables: [`columnar_wip/assemble-hier-gaps.py`](columnar_wip/assemble-hier-gaps.py) builds every table from the TSV that [`columnar_wip/tables-1042.py`](columnar_wip/tables-1042.py) `assembly` writes, now including the OO cells. The REG table comes from `tables-1042.py reg`.

> **This PR prices recorded teleportation in the columnar module merge (#1357).** `mergeLeafModulesWithinParents`, the merge pass behind module coarsening, priced every merge from link flow only. Under `--regularized` and `--recorded-teleportation`, a module's enter and exit also carry the teleport terms (T − t)·w and t·(1 − w). The move loop and the exact score include them, so the pass rejected merges the objective rewards. It now includes them too. Without recorded teleportation every added term is zero.
> - **36 of the 332 columnar configurations change, all under recorded teleportation.** The other 296 are bit-identical in codelength, top modules and levels. Over the 261 of them above 0.3G instructions, the median instruction change is +0.030% (5th–95th percentile −0.26% to +0.36%).
> - **27 get shorter in bits:**
>   - wikispeedia `-d --recorded-teleportation -N1`: −1.22%, 0.090 → 0.091 s.
>   - om2_N256_E50k `--regularized`, three rows: −0.33%.
>   - lazega with metadata, `--regularized`: −0.23% at `-N1` and −0.17% at `-N10`, 0.001 s and 0.004 s on both arms.
>   - wikispeedia `-2d --regularized -N10`: −0.15%.
> - **9 get longer, each by less than 0.02% in bits**, at instructions between −9.8% and +3.6%:
>   - om4_N256_E100k `--regularized -N10`: +0.0176% on both rows, 7.537473 → 7.538801 bits, at −2.9% and −3.6% instructions. Seeds 1 and 2 give +0.013% and −0.002%. At `-N1` the same network gets 0.030% shorter.
>   - om3_N256_E100k and om5_N256_E100k at `-N10`: +0.001% to +0.003%. Seeds 1 and 2 give −0.027% to +0.001%.
>   - wikispeedia `--recorded-teleportation` and `-d --regularized -N10`: +0.005% to +0.009%.
>
>   The pricing changes which merges a trial's coarsening accepts. A trial's endpoint moves either way by a few hundredths of a percent, and the best of ten moves with it.
> - **Seconds: one real cost, on the row with the largest gain.** om2_N256_E50k `--regularized` costs +61% / +50% in seconds at `-N1` (interleaved minimum of 5: 0.848 → 1.368 s and 1.019 → 1.528 s). Instructions move in step, +64% / +55%. At `-N10` the cost is +12% in seconds and +18% in instructions (3.27 → 3.66 s). Each buys −0.33% in bits. All of it is in the run-level deep repair, which accepts more merges: 7.6007 → 7.5636 bits against 7.6014 → 7.5888 on the old binary. The trial's time is unchanged, 0.33 s on both. wikispeedia `-2d --regularized -N10` costs +4–5% in seconds at +4.7% instructions, for its −0.15% in bits.
> - **Every other slow cell is load.** Re-timed interleaved, the 37 outliers of the sweep land between −7.2% and +7.3% in seconds wherever the instructions are flat (see [The slow cells are load](#the-slow-cells-are-load)).
> - **OO is carried, not measured.** This PR changes nothing the OO engine computes. Every OO cell is the #1168 session's, carried through #1351. The OO-vs-columnar seconds therefore compare two sessions.
>
> Measured in one session, 2026-10-20.
> - **Old:** a fresh build of the branch tip `e0caf82a` (#1351), md5 `3eccc7b2d4e7a3118c84b3534116d98a`. This is byte-identical to #1351's new arm.
> - **New:** this PR at `03643cd0`, md5 `ba3bdd9274d77e580529564c99541c64`.
> - **Load:** the 1-minute load average was around 18–21 with interactive applications busy, and no other benchmark was running. The arms are interleaved per row. `-N1` rows are the minimum of 3 reps spread across the batch.
> - **Family rows** that repeat a `-C`, `-C -2` or `-C -N1` row take that row's run, so the two tables agree.

### Every benchmark network under recorded teleportation

Every non-family network of the benchmark set under `--regularized`, and also under
`--recorded-teleportation` when it is directed, as #1163 added them. air30k `-d --regularized` is the
tables' "air30k (reg.)". multilayer and malaria are left out, because `--regularized` on multilayer input
needs the regularized-multilayer feature build. Rows run `-N10` once and `-N1` as the interleaved minimum
of 3.

Ten rows change, all on wikispeedia and lazega; every other network here is bit-identical.
- **lazega with metadata gets shorter in bits:** −0.23% at `-N1`, −0.17% at `-N10`, at 0.001 s and
  0.004 s on both arms.

  -N10` −0.15%, and the two `--regularized -N1` rows at −0.02% and −0.01%) and longer on four, each by
  less than 0.01% in bits:
  `-2d --recorded-teleportation` at `-N1` and `-N10`, `-d --recorded-teleportation -N10` and
  `-d --regularized -N10`. Those four sit at −9.8% to +3.6% in instructions.
