## Performance

> Manual old-vs-new benchmark of the `--columnar` engine over the set in [`columnar_wip/benchmark-networks.md`](columnar_wip/benchmark-networks.md). This is **not** the CI `perf-pr.yml` check, which only sees the default OO path since the new core is flag-gated.

Single-threaded (`MODE=release OPENMP=0`), `--seed 123`. Codelength in bits. **`instr` is instructions retired** (`/usr/bin/time -l`). `time` is `--timing-json`'s `timing.total_s`.
- `-N10` rows run once (deterministic; `instr` carries the comparison).
- `-N1` rows are the interleaved minimum of 3.
- Driver: [`columnar_wip/bench-sync.py`](columnar_wip/bench-sync.py) `--carry-oo`, writing [`columnar_wip/boundary-flow-ab-results.tsv`](columnar_wip/boundary-flow-ab-results.tsv), old and new on every row of the previous snapshot (#1369).
- Outliers in seconds: [`columnar_wip/retime-outliers.py`](columnar_wip/retime-outliers.py), writing [`columnar_wip/boundary-flow-retime.tsv`](columnar_wip/boundary-flow-retime.tsv).
- Tables: [`columnar_wip/assemble-hier-gaps.py`](columnar_wip/assemble-hier-gaps.py) builds every table from the TSV that [`columnar_wip/tables-1042.py`](columnar_wip/tables-1042.py) `assembly` writes, including the OO cells. The REG table comes from `tables-1042.py reg`.

> **This PR moves the columnar core's boundary flow out of the search: the groundwork for #828's regularized-multilayer prior.** The new class `ColumnarBoundaryFlow` owns three things: level aggregation; one sub-level slicer, which replaces three copies; and the recorded-teleportation state the move and merge loops keep. Levels and modules store total enter/exit (link plus teleport), as FlowCalculator computes them for leaves. Before, they stored link-only rates and recomputed the teleport closed form in five places. The teleport flow between a unit and a module now enters every move and merge delta exactly as link flow does. The teleport kernels are gone, and the move loop compiles a teleport-free body when nothing teleports. Five sub-search entry points keep the parent's link-only exit on purpose: passing the total exit is a search change, measured on its own in #1372. The finding is F73.
>
> What the snapshot shows:
> - **Columnar: 323 of 332 configurations are bit-identical** in codelength, top modules and levels. Over the 296 above 0.3G instructions, the median instruction change is −0.02% (5th–95th percentile −1.17% to +1.08%). The 99 unchanged recorded-teleportation configurations among them are at a median of −0.52%, none above +0.14%.
> - **9 configurations change, all under recorded teleportation, all by rounding.** Summing totals where the closed form used to be recomputed moves the last bits of a module rate, and on a long search that steers the trajectory. Rescoring a partition gives the same codelength under both binaries and under OO, so the objective is unchanged.
>   - 6 change in bits, each within ±0.021%: web-NotreDame `-d --recorded-teleportation` −0.021% at `-N1` and +0.016% at `-N10`, `-d --regularized -N1` −0.000002%, `-2 -d --regularized -N10` −0.0004%; wikispeedia `-d`/`-2d --recorded-teleportation -N10` −0.004%.
>   - 3 keep the same codelength with 1 to 4 fewer zero-flow top modules: word_assoc `-d --recorded-teleportation` at `-N1` and `-N10`, and wikispeedia `-2d --recorded-teleportation -N1`.
> - **Seconds:** summed over the 332 columnar configurations, −0.85% in the sweep and −2.30% with its 29 outliers re-timed interleaved (minimum of 5). The re-timed rows whose results did not change land between −4.1% and +5.0%, at −1.1% to +0.9% in instructions.
> - **Two costs remain, both on wikispeedia under `--recorded-teleportation`:**
>   - `-2d -N1` reaches the same codelength 9.1% slower in seconds and 9.3% in instructions: a different path. Over seeds 1–20 its instructions average −0.01% (−13.8% to +3.7%), so seed 123 drew a long path, not a slower code. In bits, 12 of the 20 seeds are identical and 8 change, between −0.0018% and +0.53%. Seed 10 lands in a worse basin (+0.53%), as the old binary already does at seed 123.
>   - The `-N10` rows pay +3.2% to +3.6% in seconds and +3.1% to +3.4% in instructions for −0.004% in bits. On seeds 1–8 of `-d -N10`, 7 are bit-identical at +0.36% to +2.36% in instructions (mean +1.72%). That is a cost of the new teleport path on this network, not located yet. On the other 99 unchanged recorded-teleportation configurations the same path is cheaper.
>
> Columnar arms measured in one session, 2026-10-22. OO arms carried from #1369's session, 2026-10-21, because nothing here changes what the OO engine computes or costs.
> - **Old:** a fresh build of the branch tip `fe9a21c5` (#1369), md5 `7b8bdfad0152ddf4f1f30a9560b8e5c5`. It is byte-identical to #1369's new arm.
> - **New:** `0c59ac59`, md5 `777c20dac8bb63080f7946abf64a8741`, built clean from HEAD.
> - **Load:** no other benchmark or test suite ran during the sweep or the re-timing. The arms are interleaved per row. `-N1` rows are the minimum of 3 reps spread across the batch.
> - **Family rows** that repeat a `-C`, `-C -2` or `-C -N1` row take that row's run, so the two tables agree.
